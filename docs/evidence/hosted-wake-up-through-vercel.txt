Which requests start the sleeping hosted service (Render free plan, behind Vercel)
==================================================================================

Two recordings of 2026-11-25, both made with Chromium after the service had received no request
for 18 minutes. "through Vercel" is a request to the Vercel address that Vercel forwards to the
Render service; "direct" is a request to the Render address itself. "t" counts from the moment
the browser started asking.

What the two recordings show
----------------------------

While the service was asleep, a request that Vercel forwarded to it did not start it: Render
answered each one with its 502 page. A request to the Render address itself started it, and the
store answered about 31 seconds later. In the recording of 15:07 UTC (hosted-wake-up.txt) both kinds
were sent at the same moment, which is why it did not show the difference.

Not established: why Render treats the two kinds differently (no statement about it was found in
its documentation), and whether it always does; these are two recordings from one day.
